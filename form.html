Instructions to the Nominee

This form is used by your school to identify its candidates for the Ten Outstanding Maritime Students of the Philippines (TOMSP) 2027. TOMSP recognizes maritime students who demonstrate academic and professional excellence, responsible leadership, and social responsibility.

Please note: Completing this form does not constitute a nomination. Under TOMSP rules, a student cannot self-endorse; only your school's authorized nominator may formally submit a nomination. Meeting the requirements does not guarantee selection.
  1. Nominee information. Enter your name, school, course and General Weighted Average (GWA) exactly as they appear in your official school records.
  2. Eligibility. Tick only the documents you have already submitted to your school. The evaluator will verify each item against official records.
  3. Evidence entries. For each table, enter your strongest achievements first. Three entries per table are recommended; up to five may be added using + Add row.
  4. Accuracy and honesty. State your actual role, quantify results where possible, and list only entries you can support with certificates or official records.
  5. Attachments. Provide shareable links to your Family Background Reflection, Autobiography, Maritime Project Proposal and supporting documents.
  6. Review and submit. Check all entries, confirm your consent, and submit. Your progress is saved automatically on this device until you submit, so please continue on the same browser.
